PGM BOT PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION AND OVERVIEW

This Privacy Policy describes how PGM Bot ("the Bot", "the Service", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects user information when you interact with the Bot within Discord servers. We are committed to maintaining the trust of our users and safeguarding your digital footprint. By accessing, authenticating, or using PGM Bot, you consent to the collection and data practices described in this policy.

If you do not agree with the data collection practices outlined herein, you must immediately cease all interactions with the Bot and refrain from invoking its features or services.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

PGM Bot collects only the minimal necessary data required to operate its market browsing, listing aggregation, and notification features effectively. We do not collect or store sensitive personal information such as financial details, real-life names, physical addresses, or passwords.

A. Automatically Collected Data

Discord Identifiers: Unique Discord User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, and Channel IDs. These IDs are non-sensitive numerical strings assigned by Discord used solely to recognize your account and route marketplace data correctly.

Listing & Market Data: Item titles, descriptions, price values, and metadata included within market listings generated or searched through PGM Bot commands.

Usage Logs: Command usage timestamps, error logs, and system performance metrics used to monitor rate limits, fix technical bugs, and optimize performance.

B. Data We DO NOT Collect

Private Messages: We do not read, log, or monitor private Direct Messages between users outside of commands explicitly sent directly to the Bot.

General Channel Chat: We do not read or record general conversation text within Discord channels. The Bot only processes messages directed at its specific slash commands or designated marketplace channels.

Payment Credentials: All commercial transactions are handled externally by users or third-party platforms. PGM Bot never collects, stores, or processes credit card details, bank info, or payment accounts.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We strictly utilize collected data to provide, maintain, and improve the functionality of PGM Bot. Specific operational uses include:

Market Aggregation: Indexing user-submitted market listings to allow cross-channel search and item comparison across participating servers.

Notification Services: Dispatching real-time notifications or watchlist alerts via Direct Messages when an item matching your criteria is posted.

Reputation & Fraud Prevention: Tracking historical trading activity within the Bot to compute user reputation metrics and deter fraudulent behavior.

System Maintenance: Analyzing performance logs to resolve crashes, prevent system exploits, and enforce rate-limiting rules.

4. DATA STORAGE, RETENTION, AND SECURITY

Storage Infrastructure: Collected data is stored in secure, restricted-access database servers. We employ industry-standard encryption protocols during data transit and at rest.

Data Retention Limits: Marketplace listing data is automatically purged or archived after it expires, becomes irrelevant, or is deleted by the seller. Operational logs are regularly rotated and permanently destroyed after thirty (30) days.

Security Protocols: Access to database records is strictly limited to authorized developers of PGM Bot. While we employ rigorous administrative and technical safeguards, no electronic storage method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute immunity against unauthorized intrusion.

5. THIRD-PARTY DATA SHARING

We respect your privacy and adhere to a strict policy regarding third-party sharing:

No Data Sales: We do never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data or market activity logs to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

Service Integrations: Data is processed through Discord’s API ecosystem in accordance with Discord's Developer Terms.

Legal Compliance: We may disclose collected information if required to do so by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of PGM Bot, its users, or the general public.

6. DATA CONTROL, DELETION, AND YOUR RIGHTS

You retain full control over your data stored within PGM Bot's infrastructure.

Command-Based Deletion: Removing a listing or running user-deletion commands provided by the Bot will immediately mark your associated listing data for deletion.

Manual Data Removal Requests: You have the right to request the complete deletion of all your stored user data from our databases. To submit a removal request, contact the Bot support team or server administrators. Upon verification, your stored data will be permanently wiped within seven (7) business days.

Removing the Bot: Server administrators can remove or ban PGM Bot from their Discord server at any time, which halts all data collection for that server immediately.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (STRICT 18+ ENFORCEMENT)

As stated in our Terms of Service, PGM Bot is strictly restricted to users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected data from a minor without verification, that data will be promptly expunged from our system.

8. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We reserve the right to revise or update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be published directly to this document with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top. Your continued use of PGM Bot following any policy modifications constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
